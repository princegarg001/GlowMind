# 🎵 Sound Therapy Feature - Quick Start Guide

## What is Sound Therapy?

GlowMind now includes a Sound Therapy feature that provides curated playlists of calming sounds tailored to your current emotional state. Each playlist contains 10 carefully selected ambient sounds from FreeSound.org designed to help you relax, focus, and restore balance.

## How to Use

### 1. Open Sound Therapy
- Launch GlowMind
- On the home page, look for the **"Soothing Sounds"** button (below "Start Glow Ritual")
- Tap the button to open your mood-appropriate playlist

### 2. Playback Controls

**Play/Pause**
- Large circular button in the center
- Tap to start or pause playback

**Skip Tracks**
- Previous: Left arrow button
- Next: Right arrow button

**Shuffle**
- Left-most button with shuffle icon
- When active, tracks play in random order

**Loop Mode**
- Right-most button with repeat icon
- Tap to cycle through:
  - No Loop (plays once and stops)
  - Loop All (repeats entire playlist)
  - Loop One (repeats current track)

**Progress Bar**
- Shows current playback position
- Drag to seek to any point in the track

**Track Selection**
- Tap any track in the list to play it immediately

### 3. Sleep Timer
- Tap the timer icon (⏱) in the top right
- Choose from preset durations or set custom time
- Audio fades out 30 seconds before timer expires
- Perfect for falling asleep to soothing sounds

## Tips for Best Experience

1. **Use Headphones**: For immersive experience, especially with binaural beats
2. **Set Timer**: If using before sleep, set the sleep timer
3. **Lower Volume**: Keep volume comfortable but not distracting
4. **Dark Room**: Combine with dimmed lights for maximum relaxation
5. **Regular Use**: Make it part of your daily routine

## Troubleshooting

**No sounds playing?**
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure volume is not muted
- Try tapping the retry button if error appears

**Sounds loading slowly?**
- Wait a few moments for stream URLs to load
- Check your network speed
- Try closing and reopening the playlist

**Wrong mood playlist?**
- The playlist matches your current glow state
- To change, adjust your mood on the home page first
- Then open sound therapy again
